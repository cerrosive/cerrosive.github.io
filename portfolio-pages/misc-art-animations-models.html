  • lootbox project

loot box

One of my assignments was to design a 3D lootbox design, I decided to base mine off of hollow knight items, which I ended up settling on the godtuner item.

We had to make the lootbox in blender, my design was composed of multiple parts:"

  • box body
  • 3 lid parts
  • locl front
  • lock light
  • line lights

Texturing I used a free gold texture found online as well as using emissives in blender for the lights, I also used a HDRI to test lighting better.

I then keyframed each part of the lootbox to make an opening and closing animation.

Due to visibility keyframes not transferring to Unity, for the lights I had to keyframe them to position inside the box then move them out so they are visible.

When I put the lootbox into unity I had to fix all the textures manually as they did not properly transfer over. I created a gold texture and used the original texture's files to remake it as best I could in the world's lighting, and for the lights I just made a new emissive texture.

For this method of animating since the lootbox was made up of multiple pieces, I made it all as a single animation which I then cut up inside of Unity.

Once animations were set up to play when clicked, I then added a particle effect to give a sort of glowing/shining effect coming out of the box, as particles were one of the requirements.

These particles were achieved by making a circle particle, changing the settings to remove the grey ring, stretching them really long, setting the movement upwards and tweaking the settings until it looked right.

You can see that the lock light isn't functional, I am unsure why this has happened however the model for the lock's light did not import into Unity for whatever reason, and due to time constraints in the project I decided to leave it.
