lore and worldbuilding

Redline is one of my most heavily developed stories, the game is intended to be quite story-driven and have a lot of lore, so I've put much effort into building the world for it:

plot summary

You are an employee hired for one job: exterminate the creatures deep within the facility. The world is at its end after these monsters had overridden the main lands, driving people out of their homes and into isolation. The company you work for, the Nullpoint Syndicate, aims to minimize the damage and lower the risk for the population that remains, so they send you to the source: the Apex Black site. Your aim is to kill off as many creatures as possible, with a specific quota. Beat the game, and you are granted a life of peace covered by the company, or so they say.

lore summary

TL;DR if you want to skip the paragraphs:

  • The blacksite used to be ran by the A.E.C attempting to create perfect humans through experiments
  • Said experiments created the mutants that caused the apocalypse
  • Nullpoint has ulterior motives for trying to weaponise the mutants and continue their research
  • the core/the mother is the primary source of the mutants, a massive mutation with other mutants being created from it
  • Nullpoint's knowledge on the core is incredibly limited as it's in the deepest part of the blacksite

The Apex Black site was a secret government-funded operation ran by the Apex Echelon Corporation (A.E.C). The foundation was responsible for various mutation experiments and research, and was heavily kept under wraps by the government to avoid knowledge to the public or release of classified data. The goal of these experiments was to speed up human evolution and learn methods of how the human body could be improved and taken further, essentially to create the "perfect human".

Eventually however, one particular experiment would go horribly wrong, birthing the creatures that would soon after rampage the facility, leaving it in ruin, before moving to the surface wreaking havoc and causing the apocalypse. Their "perfect humans" would become the demise of humanity, an ironic end to such a huge government campaign.

In comes the Nullpoint Syndicate, a company founded following the collapse of society since the start of the apocalypse. Given the severity of the situation, the company rose up and took charge for the protection of the remaining population and containing the creatures as well as their source. The company has various divisions, the main one in the front lines known as the "Redline Division", made up of death-row criminals and those without a chance at life whom they send on extermination expeditions into the Apex Black site. Volunteers are also welcome to join the expeditions, though you could imagine there's only so many willing to do so.

However, the Nullpoint Syndicate is a bit more sinister than what meets the eye. Following the apocalypse, the company did anything and everything in their power to keep A.E.C and the incident under wraps. With the government collapsed, the company now holds all records, data, and documents related to Apex Echelon, and has kept the secret that the creatures, the "perfect humans" as they are referred to in the files, were a result of an experiment gone wrong. Their motif for this is because Nullpoint in reality wants power and control. Hiding the details of Apex Echelon allows them to control the masses and how much information is revealed, being able to tailor the story to make the population have their utmost trust in them. Additionally, documents talk of the pathogen "E19", which is emitted off of the core and some deceased creatures. E19 causes rapid and detrimental mutations in the body, essentially destroying it from the inside out, and is easily fatal. Nullpoint, however, believes there's a way to weaponize it and use it to get what they want. This is why they keep all the information on Apex Echelon classified; to hide their true motives and control the masses.

Then there's the core. It's been mentioned here and there, but what exactly is it? The core, also known as the Mother, is the source of the perfect humans. The original experiment that resulted in disaster had created a large mutation, a creature far beyond what was intended by the original project and a monster way past the control of the A.E.C. It is the largest and most dangerous creature of all, and has the ability to create more creatures that stem off of it, the only way these perfect humans can multiply, and hence its title "the Mother". It resides in the original holding chamber where the experiment took place, now transformed into a large nest. The employees are fully unaware of the details of this "core", and even Nullpoint's knowledge on it is limited due to little data kept about it.

art and visuals

concept art

Based on the story I had created I knew what kind of aesthetic I wanted. I was thinking dark, industrial, apocalyptic, something along those lines.

A list of my primary inspirations:

  • Pressure (roblox game)
  • ULTRAKILL
  • Lethal Company

I drew up some concept art for what I had in mind for the main character, D-24.

I did a lot of research into hazmat suits to ensure the design was sensible, and I also made a list of considerations for the environment of my game so the design would make sense in it:

  • decrease of oxygen the lower the floor level
  • toxins and contaminants
  • toxic air
  • dark areas
  • enemies and environmental hazards

In the end I did a combination of concepts #1 and #3.
