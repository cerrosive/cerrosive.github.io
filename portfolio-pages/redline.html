art and visuals

Based on the story I had created I knew what kind of aesthetic I wanted. I was thinking dark, industrial, apocalyptic, something along those lines.

A list of my primary inspirations:

  • Pressure (roblox game)
  • ULTRAKILL
  • Lethal Company

I drew up some concept art for what I had in mind for the main character, D-24.

I did a lot of research into hazmat suits to ensure the design was sensible, and I also made a list of considerations for the environment of my game so the design would make sense in it:

  • decrease of oxygen the lower the floor level
  • toxins and contaminants
  • toxic air
  • dark areas
  • enemies and environmental hazards

In the end I did a combination of concepts #1 and #3.
