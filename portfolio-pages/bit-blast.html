bit blast

  • genre: micrograme
  • engine: Unity
  • time: 2 months
  • platform: browser-based

game design

For our second major assignment we were tasked with building a microgame, similar to warioware. this makes the task simple, all i need is a simple minigame with up to 3 levels that happens within the span of roughly 20 seconds, give or take.

brainstorming

I needed to first figure out exactly what I wanted the microgame to be, I had a few options so I put them all in a table.

Given I only had limited time until the deadline to make this game, I needed to choose something that would be manageable within the timeframe I had.

I ended up colourcoding them in order of priority, as I was allowed to make more than one microgame if I had enough time. I ended up settling with the space invaders-style game as it was the simplest for me to make while still being quite fitting.

designing the game

I quickly sketched out the idea I had in mind for the level and game overall.

This game would only have 2 scenes, an intermission and then of course the actual game.

The minigame is quite simple, just a set of enemies that you have to aim at via the mouse and shoot, with each level, going up to 3, increasing the amount of enemies.

art and visuals

I knew from the start what kind of art style I wanted. I was particularly inspired by those little alien creatures you find around the internet, so I wanted to replicate that in this game. Pixel art was what we have been focusing on in our classes, hence it was the medium I chose for the visuals.

concept art

This was mainly to get sketches of the main characters down, however simple, and sketch out my ideas for the layout and gameplay.

I thought a fun way to show the countdown towards the minigame would be through the ship's console, which you can see in my sketches the rough idea for it. I also have a small sketch for the minigame itself. which is essentially a simplified version of space invaders.

sprite art

first I wanted to tackle the sprites that would actually be in the minigame, so I quickly drew these small simple sprites, the first for the player and the second for the enemy."

I also made an animation for when the player successfully hits an enemy ship.

I also needed to create some tilesets for the environment, so i created a simple starry background and a border that mimics the terminal, as you are meant to be controlling it from the terminal in-game.

then I needed animations for the "intermission", in other words the part between the microgame levels.

I made 3 animations for this, a countdown to the level, and a win and lose screen.

time to get into extra details, starting with this opening and closing animation for the start and end of the microgame, mirroring the intermission animations at the terminal.

the terminal opens at the start of the microgame, and closes at the end. this is to give a more polished transition

I also made this tank animation which functions as a visual timer during the microgame. it is meant to represent fuel in the ship.

finally some last touch details

I made these heart sprites that function as health and sit at the bottom during intermission sections.

And I also made a quick image to indicate to the player what to do during the minigame itself.
